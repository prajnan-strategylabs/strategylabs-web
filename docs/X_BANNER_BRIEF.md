# X (Twitter) Banner Brief — Strategy Labs

Paste this prompt into Canva's text-to-design / AI generator, hand it to a designer, or hand it to your Cowork Marketing Lead session (Canva connector). The output is a single 1500×500 PNG ready to upload as the X header.

---

```
Design an X (Twitter) header banner for Strategy Labs — a crypto backtesting-engine
SaaS. Premium, quant, calm. Think Bloomberg terminal meets Linear meets a hedge-fund
pitch deck. Not a Robinhood ad.

CANVAS
- 1500 × 500 px exactly (X header spec)
- Profile picture overlays bottom-left, roughly 80–280 px from left edge / 280–480 px
  from top (a circle ~200 px diameter). Keep that quadrant CLEAR of critical text.
- Safe zone for crucial content: from x≈360 onward, y≈40 to y≈460. On small viewports
  X crops the top and bottom — anything must-see should sit in the middle 320 px tall.

BRAND
- Background: deep navy #0a0e1a (primary). Layer #0f1525 for card surfaces.
- Mint accent: #22d3aa (the ONLY bright color — use sparingly for the equity curve,
  numbers, and one underline accent). No other neon.
- Text: #e6e9f0 (primary), #8b94a8 (muted/secondary)
- Subtle line color: #1e2740
- Fonts: Inter (or Inter Tight) for display + body. JetBrains Mono for any numbers
  or code-like elements.

LAYOUT (right two-thirds of canvas)
- Headline, centered or right-aligned, very large bold Inter Tight, tracked tight:
    "Test any trading idea in 30 seconds."
  (Color: #e6e9f0. ~56–64 px font size.)

- Subhead directly below in muted ink (~18 px, Inter, weight 500):
    "Plain-English crypto backtest engine · 9-year audited proof"

- Credibility strip below or right-aligned, tiny mono caps (~12 px, JetBrains Mono,
  letter-spacing ~0.18em, color #8b94a8 with mint highlights on the numbers):
    "+953% AUDITED  ·  2,410 TRADES  ·  49.3% WIN  ·  SHARPE 2.84"

BACKGROUND TEXTURE (subtle, behind the text)
- A thin mint equity curve (#22d3aa, 2 px stroke) sweeping from bottom-left to
  top-right of the canvas, with a soft area-fill gradient under it fading to
  transparent. Looks like a V22 audit curve.
- Faint dotted/dashed horizontal grid lines (#1e2740 at 30% opacity) — Bloomberg vibe.
- A radial gradient glow behind the headline center: rgba(34,211,170, 0.08) → transparent.
- OPTIONAL micro-detail in the left-bottom area BELOW the profile-pic zone: a tiny
  monospace code snippet ghosted at ~15% opacity, e.g.:
    "// strategy = 'RSI(14) < 30 AND trend.daily == bull'"
    "// compiling..."
    "// backtest: 9.0y, 47 pairs → +953% (audited)"
  Treat as texture, not content. Keep readable but recessive.

DO NOT INCLUDE
- Rockets 🚀, moons 🌙, lambos, fire 🔥, money bags 💰 — none of it.
- Bright yellow / orange / red gradients. Crypto-bro neon. Hexagonal "metaverse" tech motifs.
- Bitcoin coins floating in hands / generic crypto stock photography.
- Candlestick charts tilted dramatically up.
- The word "AI", "guaranteed", "100%", "secret", "exclusive".

VIBE WORDS
calm · audited · transparent · quant · grown-up · receipts-based · earned-trust.

DELIVERABLE
Single 1500×500 PNG, no transparency. Export at full resolution.
```

---

## Variants worth generating

If Canva or the AI gives you the choice, ask for 3 variants:

1. **Receipt-heavy** — credibility strip is bigger, equity curve dominates the background, headline smaller. For traders who care about numbers.
2. **Hook-heavy** — headline dominates ("Test any trading idea in 30 seconds."), curve + numbers recede. For the curious-tinkerer persona.
3. **Demo-screenshot** — replace the background with a tasteful, blurred mockup of the Strategy Lab UI (the compile pipeline). Risk: looks busier; reward: doubles as a product preview.

Pick the variant that best balances "instantly says what we do" with "doesn't feel like an ad."

## Alt copy you can swap in

If "Test any trading idea in 30 seconds." feels too long for your face:

- *Headline:* `Backtest. Validate. Trade.`
  *Sub:* `9 years of audited crypto edge. In plain English.`

- *Headline:* `Show me the backtest.`
  *Sub:* `Strategy Labs — plain-English crypto backtest engine.`

- *Headline:* `Stop guessing.`
  *Sub:* `Test any crypto trading idea against 9 years of data.`

(The first is sharpest — keep it unless the type-size becomes an issue.)
